### Projects

**[GDP growth forecasting](https://github.com/nekumonokat/ACCT653_FFA_Forecasting_Project)** &nbsp;`pandas` `xgboost` `statsmodels`<br>
Ridge, Random Forest and XGBoost ensemble forecasting US quarterly GDP growth, and a test of whether accounting data adds any signal. Team project.

**[Evolving virtual creatures](https://github.com/nekumonokat/AI_pybullet)** &nbsp;`numpy` `pybullet` `unittest`<br>
A genetic algorithm that evolves creatures to move in a physics simulation, backed by 49 unit tests.

**[Mammogram classification](https://github.com/nekumonokat/FYP_breast_cancer_classification)** &nbsp;`tensorflow` `opencv`<br>
A CNN that sorts CBIS-DDSM mammograms into benign and malignant, then cuts overfitting with augmentation and tuning. Final-year project.

**[Twitter sentiment analysis](https://github.com/nekumonokat/NLP_text_classification)** &nbsp;`nltk` `scikit-learn`<br>
TF-IDF with Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes and SVM, rebuilt across three datasets after size, skew and bias problems.

**[kNN vs decision tree](https://github.com/nekumonokat/ML_midterm_KNN_algo)** &nbsp;`python` `scikit-learn`<br>
Both classifiers built from scratch and compared on handwritten digits. kNN with Euclidean distance won.

**[CIFAR-10 with Keras](https://github.com/nekumonokat/ML_finals_project)** &nbsp;`tensorflow` `keras`<br>
Deep-learning experiments working through dropout and batch normalisation against overfitting.
